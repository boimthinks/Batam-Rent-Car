## Pickup Protocol at Batam Centre Ferry Terminal

Batam Centre Ferry Terminal is the international gateway for travellers from HarbourFront Singapore and Stulang Laut Malaysia. Vehicle handover here takes 10 to 15 minutes, and the delivery fee is free.

The process is straightforward:

1. Our team waits in the arrival lobby and the outdoor terminal parking area, near Mega Mall Batam Centre, Batam Mayor's Office, Batam Grand Mosque, and the BP Batam building.
2. Key handover and a physical vehicle inspection take 5 to 10 minutes.
3. If the customs or immigration queue is long, the chauffeur waits inside the car with the air conditioning running instead of standing at the gate. Passengers do not have to wait in the heat and humidity.

The 10 to 15 minute estimate applies on normal days. During long weekends and public holidays the Singapore side gets crowded and handover can take longer. The fix is simple: send us your arrival schedule as early as possible so the car is already in position before you clear inspection.

## Before You Board the Ferry

Guests from Singapore and Malaysia should carry a passport valid for at least six months, plus a return ferry ticket or hotel booking confirmation. Those documents are needed for terminal verification.

If you want to rent a car and drive yourself, the requirements are an International Driving Permit or a valid domestic licence from your home country. For the Alphard those requirements do not apply, because a chauffeur is included. What we need from you is the arrival schedule, vessel name, and luggage count, and that is enough for us to position the car at the terminal.
