## Vehicle Cargo Engineering and Load Management

Transporting heavy grocery hauls requires basic vehicle weight distribution. Carrying several dozen liters of laundry detergent, cooking oil cans, and preserved jarred foods can easily add hundreds of kilograms to the trunk.

To preserve vehicle stability and stay well within the Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR), heavy cargo must sit directly over or forward of the rear axle. Drivers ensure the heaviest boxes stay on the floor pan rather than stacked near the tailgate.

### Flat-Bed Cargo Configuration and Cabin Protection

Maximizing storage volume requires folding the third-row seats flat. The tumble-fold mechanism in modern MPVs creates an expansive, level surface for stacking uniform cardboard boxes.

Spills and strong odors present a real hazard when transporting regional food products. Our operations team equips shopping rental vehicles with heavy-duty tarpaulin sheets across the luggage floor. This protective waterproof liner prevents soy sauce leaks, cooking oil residue, or pungent smells from shrimp paste and dried anchovies from penetrating the vehicle upholstery.

### Basic Cold Chain Management

Temperature-sensitive groceries like regional butter blocks, traditional spreads, and chilled processed foods need basic thermal protection in tropical weather. We recommend bringing along a soft-sided or hard cooler box with frozen ice gel packs.

The steady cabin air conditioning in our fleet keeps ambient temperatures cool between destinations. Storing cooler boxes in the middle passenger footwell shields them from direct rear windshield sunlight.

To clear your entire shopping checklist before your afternoon ferry departs, follow this field-tested stopover schedule.

Your designated driver meets you at Harbour Bay Ferry Terminal or Batam Centre Ferry Terminal arrival lobby. Inspect the trunk area together, lay down the cargo tarp, and load your empty folding luggage carts into the vehicle.

### 16:00 - 16:30 WIB: Ferry Terminal Arrival and Staging
Arrive at your departure ferry terminal at least 90 minutes before sailing time. Your driver assists in transferring boxes onto your personal luggage trolleys and double-checks that all receipts are accessible for customs clearance.

## Cross-Border Compliance: Singapore Customs and SFA Guidelines

Crossing the Singapore Strait with grocery hauls demands full awareness of import limitations to avoid border fines and baggage confiscation.

### Singapore GST Relief Thresholds
Travelers spending less than 48 hours outside Singapore receive a GST relief allowance of SGD 100 per adult traveler. Keep all paper tax receipts accessible on the vehicle dashboard and declare taxable items through the official Custom@SG mobile application before reaching immigration check counters.

### Singapore Food Agency (SFA) Limits
The Singapore Food Agency strictly regulates personal food imports. Each passenger may bring a maximum of 5 kilograms or 5 liters of processed food products. Fresh raw poultry, uncertified beef, fresh bird eggs, and unsealed food items are strictly prohibited. All sauces and preserved snacks must arrive in intact manufacturer packaging with clear ingredient labels.

### Indonesian Customs Export Clearance
Indonesian customs officers at the ferry departure gate monitor baggage volume to differentiate between bona fide personal consumption and undeclared commercial freight. Keeping grocery items organized into individual traveler allowances prevents commercial cargo detention.
