Just a 45-minute ferry ride from Singapore HarbourFront or Tanah Merah, Batam Island is a favourite weekend escape for shopping, seafood feasts, and golf getaways. Having your own private car provides unmatched flexibility compared to relying on point-to-point taxis.

Here is a practical guide by Lincah Rent Car for travellers coming from Singapore and Malaysia.

### 1. Can Singaporeans and Malaysians Rent a Self-Drive Car in Batam?
Yes! You can legally drive in Batam with your valid Singapore Driving Licence or Malaysian Driving License (or an International Driving Permit). Driving in Indonesia is on the **left side of the road** (right-hand drive vehicles), exactly identical to Singapore and Malaysia.

### 2. Required Documents for Booking:
- Clear photo of your Passport (valid for at least 6 months).
- Valid Driving Licence (Singapore Class 3 / Malaysian Class D).
- Return Ferry Ticket confirmation.
- Hotel or resort accommodation booking details.

### 3. Recommended Cars for Island Exploration
- **Toyota New Avanza (~SGD 27 / day)**: Great for couples and small families navigating city streets and shopping malls.
- **Toyota Innova Reborn (~SGD 45 / day)**: Superior comfort and powerful engine for drives to Barelang Bridge and beach resorts.
- **Toyota Alphard VIP (~SGD 195 / day with driver)**: Top choice for VIP delegates, golf groups, and special events.

### 4. Seamless Ferry Terminal Handover
Our representative will meet you at the arrival hall of Harbour Bay Ferry Terminal or Batam Centre Ferry Terminal with the vehicle ready. Key handover and quick exterior inspection take less than 10 minutes.
